Skeleton

The Skeleton is a common hostile undead mob in Minecraft known for its ranged combat abilities. They spawn in dark areas of the Overworld at night or in light levels of zero, as well as in Soul Sand Valleys in the Nether. Skeletons have 20 health points (10 hearts) and are equipped with a bow, firing arrows at players and other targets from a distance with high accuracy.

Skeletons burn in daylight unless they are in water, under shade, or wearing a helmet. Their main danger comes from their ability to strafe while shooting, making it difficult to hit them with ranged weapons of your own. Additionally, their arrows deal knockback, which can push players off edges or into hazardous environments like lava.

suggestion: To counter a skeleton, always carry a Shield, which completely negates arrow damage and deflects incoming shots. You can either use obstacles to block their line of sight and force them into close range, or dodge in a zig-zag pattern while closing the gap. Weapons with the Smite enchantment are extremely effective against them, and using a bow with Projectile Protection armor can greatly reduce the danger in open terrain.
