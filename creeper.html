Creeper

The Creeper is a famous stealthy hostile mob in Minecraft. Spawning in dark areas at night or in caves with light levels of zero, it possesses 20 health points (10 hearts). Unlike most other mobs, the Creeper moves completely silently until it gets close to a player, at which point it emits a short, distinct hissing sound before detonating itself, dealing severe area-of-effect damage and destroying surrounding terrain.

Creepers are uniquely dangerous because they do not burn in sunlight, allowing them to linger around player bases long after night ends. Additionally, if a Creeper is struck by lightning, it transforms into a Charged Creeper, greatly increasing its explosion power and blast radius.

suggestion: To deal with a Creeper, the Shield is your best friend, as holding it up completely blocks all explosion damage. A common melee technique is the "hit and knockback" method: hit the Creeper once, quickly jump back to reset its fuse timer, and repeat until defeated. Using a bow or crossbow from a distance is the safest strategy. Furthermore, Creepers are terrified of cats and ocelots, making tamed felines an excellent defense system for your home base.
