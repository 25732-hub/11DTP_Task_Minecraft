Enderman

The Enderman is a tall, neutral-turned-hostile mob found in the Overworld, the Nether, and predominantly the End. Possessing 40 health points (20 hearts), an Enderman remains passive unless a player looks directly into its eyes or attacks it first. Once provoked, it emits a loud, distorted screech, opens its mouth, and teleports toward the player to deal heavy melee damage.

Endermen have several unique traits and weaknesses: they are damaged by water and rain, cannot be hit by projectiles such as arrows or snowballs due to their instant teleportation, and can pick up and place certain blocks.

suggestion: To fight an Enderman safely, build a 3x3 horizontal platform that is exactly two blocks high off the ground. Since Endermen are three blocks tall, they cannot fit under the shelter, allowing you to attack their legs with complete immunity. Alternatively, you can stand in a body of water, as they will take damage and teleport away upon touching it. Putting on a Carved Pumpkin as a helmet prevents them from becoming hostile when you look at them, though it reduces your screen visibility.
