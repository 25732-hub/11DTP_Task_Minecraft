Drowned

Base stats:
The Drowned is an underwater variant of the zombie found in oceans, rivers, and dripstone caves, or created when a normal zombie submerges in water for too long. They possess 20 health points (10 hearts) and can move freely underwater. While mostly passive during the day on land, they are hostile in water or at night, attacking players on sight.

Mob characteristics:
What makes Drowned particularly dangerous is that some spawn equipped with a Trident. A Trident-wielding Drowned can throw tridents from a distance, dealing heavy damage both in water and on land. Drowned can also spawn holding nautilus shells or fishing rods, making them a great source for rare early-game loot.

suggestion:
To defeat Drowned, fighting them on land or near the surface gives you a mobility advantage, as swimming limits your movement. Using a Shield is vital to block incoming thrown tridents. Standard swords with the Smite enchantment work exceptionally well against them since they are classified as undead. If you need to fight underwater, using a weapon with Impaling or wearing armor with the Respiration and Depth Strider enchantments will make aquatic combat significantly easier.
