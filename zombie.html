Zombie

Base stats:
The Zombie is one of the most iconic hostile undead mobs in Minecraft. They spawn at night or in dimly lit area where the light level is zero. A standard zombie possesses 20 health points (10 hearts) and attacks players, villagers, and iron golems on sight through melee contact.

Mob characteristics:
When fighting zombies, keep in mind that they are vulnerable to daylight and will catch fire unless they are wearing headgear or submerged in water. However, beware of Baby Zombies, which do not burn in sunlight, move significantly faster, and have smaller hitboxes. In higher difficulty modes, zombies can call for reinforcements when damaged, potentially swarming you if you stay in one place for too long.

suggestion:
To defeat zombies efficiently, use a sword enchanted with Smite, as it deals massive extra damage to all undead creatures. A standard Shield can completely block their attacks. If you find yourself overwhelmed, simply place two or three blocks beneath you to elevate yourself; zombies cannot reach you at this height, allowing you to safely deal with them from above.
