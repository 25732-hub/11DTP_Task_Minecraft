Spider

Base stats:
The Spider is a common, highly agile mob that spawns in light levels of zero or throughout the Overworld at night. Possessing 16 health points (8 hearts), Spiders are unique because they are neutral in bright light (light level 12 or higher) but become hostile at night or in dark caves.

Mob characteristics:
Spiders have several distinct abilities: they can climb vertical walls and surfaces, move faster than most basic mobs, and jump toward players to deal melee damage. They are also completely immune to the Slowness status effect and poison. Additionally, there is a small chance for a Spider to spawn with a Skeleton riding its back, forming a dangerous Spider Jockey.

suggestion:
To combat Spiders effectively, keep your distance using a bow or hit them mid-air when they jump at you to reset their momentum. Weapons enchanted with Bane of Arthropods deal massive extra damage to Spiders and apply a Slowness effect, making them easy to take down. Building overhangs or lips on walls (such as placing slabs or stairs facing outward) stops them from climbing over your bases.
