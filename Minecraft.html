About Minecraft

Minecraft is a sandbox video game developed by Mojang Studios. In Minecraft, players explore a blocky, procedurally generated 3D world with virtually infinite terrain, and may discover and extract raw materials, craft tools and items, and build structures.

About Hostile Mobs

Hostile mobs are dangerous entities that will attack players whenever they are nearby. Each hostile mob has unique behaviors, attack styles, and fatal weaknesses. Surviving the night requires mastering how to deal with them.
